# Pure Spoiler Shield — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 6, 2026_

Pure Spoiler Shield is a browser extension that hides possible spoilers for games, shows, films and anime (by default for GTA VI) on web pages. It works entirely inside your browser.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Pure Spoiler Shield does not collect, sell or share any personal data. We have no servers that receive your data, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no third-party tracking code.

## What the extension can access

- **Free version: YouTube, Reddit and X (Twitter) only.** On these sites it reads the text of the page (titles, descriptions, comments, labels) inside your browser to decide what to blur.
- **Google, TikTok and Instagram — only if you press "Turn on there too":** your browser asks you once to allow these sites. Then the same check runs there. You can remove the access at any time in the browser's extension settings.
- **Pro, only if you turn "Protect every site" on:** your browser asks you to allow access to all websites. Then the same check runs on every site you open. You can switch it off at any time; the access can also be removed in the browser's extension settings.
- It does not read passwords or form fields, does not see your browsing history, and does not store anything about the pages you visit. Blocks that contain a text field or editor are never covered.

## What stays on your device

- Your settings (on/off, the titles and words you protect and their level and dates, folded comments, paused time, sites where it is off, "protect until" date, trusted and always-hidden authors). Words you hide with the right-click menu are saved the same way.
- A counter of how many blocks were hidden in total (a number only).
- License status if you activated Pro.
- All of this is in the browser's local extension storage and is removed together with the extension.

## Network requests

The extension itself makes one kind of network request: when you enter a license key bought on Gumroad, it sends that key and our product ID to Gumroad's license API (`api.gumroad.com`) to check the purchase, and re-checks it at most every 7 days. No page text, no browsing data and no settings are ever sent. Gumroad's own privacy policy applies to your purchase.

## Reports you send yourself

- **"Not a spoiler? Report"** (right-click menu) opens a new GitHub issue page in a tab, pre-filled with the list name, the site, the extension version and the text of the block you clicked. Nothing is sent until you read it and press "Submit" on GitHub yourself; you can edit or delete any part first. GitHub's privacy policy applies to what you publish there.
- **"Copy a report"** (shown only when a site seems to have changed its layout) copies numbers only — version, site, how many cards and links were found, how many blocks were hidden — to your clipboard. No page text.
